## 01 · The threat: keyed in under two minutes

Modern Falcon theft doesn't need a smashed window and a screwdriver. The immobiliser (PATS) is unlocked through the **instrument cluster**, and the security handshake it uses is well known to commonly available aftermarket key-programming tools.

Plug in at the OBD port, pull security access from the cluster, program a blank key, and the car simply drives away. It is quiet, fast, and leaves almost no trace.

**The cluster is the weak link — so the cluster is what we harden.**

![FG Falcon — commonly targeted](163706907-48fcd541-6998-42c8-a673-b33784e09128.png)

## 02 · The fix: lock the cluster read-only

The upgrade rewrites the VDO cluster's firmware so it **refuses the security-access handshake** that aftermarket diagnostic software and scan tools depend on.

In practical terms the cluster becomes **locked — effectively read-only** to that tooling: no key programming, no PATS parameter resets, no elevated diagnostic writes. That is exactly what stops a thief from plugging in and programming a fresh key.

- ✓ The parts of the cluster needed to recover it are never touched.
- ✓ Your dash, odometer, gauges and warning lights work exactly as before.
- ✓ Reversible by authorised means — see the workshop directory below.

## 03 · Download & run the software

The TESTER Anti-Theft Flasher is a free Windows application. It does the whole job for you: it downloads the hardened firmware securely at flash time and writes it to your cluster locally.

**[⤓ Download TESTER Anti-Theft Flasher](download/TesterAntiTheftFlasher-Setup.exe)** — Windows 10/11

> **Requirements:** Windows 10/11 PC · a J2534 PassThru interface · access to the OBD-II port (including the brown B+ connector)

**Steps**

1. **Install & open** the TESTER Anti-Theft Flasher, and accept the on-screen terms.
2. **Plug in your J2534 device** to the PC and to the car's OBD-II port. Keep a battery charger / stable power on the vehicle.
3. **Select your device and press CONNECT.**
4. **Press FLASH & PROTECT.** The hardened firmware is downloaded and verified in memory — nothing is stored on your PC.
5. **Cycle the brown plug when prompted** (unplug 3 seconds, plug back in). The tool does the rest.
6. **Done.** *"Your cluster is now protected."* Your Falcon is now locked against aftermarket key programming.

> ⚠️ **Do it right:** keep the vehicle on stable power and do not disconnect the device or the ignition once the upgrade has begun. Reflashing a cluster is not risk-free — perform it on a healthy battery/charger, and if you are not confident, have one of the workshops below do it for you.
